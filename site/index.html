Unofficial · not affiliated with GoogleGoogle Home Volume Sync

Keep google home speaker and device volumes in sync with the program running on your Windows computer. It looks for changes in volume in devices, and matches the volume on devices you assign in this program. You can also adjust relative volume gain to balance volumes as desired throughout your home.

What it does

Finds every Cast device

Google Home, Nest Mini, Nest Audio, Nest Hub, Chromecasts and cast groups appear automatically.

Sync groups

Group any speakers; change one volume anywhere (app, phone, or the speaker itself) and the rest follow instantly.

Sync Gain balancing

Set a per-device 0–200% gain so quiet corners get boosted and loud rooms stay tame.

System tray presets

Right-click the tray icon and set any group to 5%…100% in two clicks, plus play/pause controls.

Scheduler

Quiet the house at bedtime: weekly day/time rules that set volumes for groups or single devices.

More than Google

Also controls Roku TVs, Yamaha MusicCast receivers, LG webOS TVs and Optoma projectors, in the same sync groups.

Roku is tested on real hardware. The Yamaha, LG and Optoma backends are written to spec but untested — reports welcome.
