What it does

One always-on PC, every speaker in step. Nothing leaves your network.

🔍
Finds every Cast device

Google Home, Nest Mini, Nest Audio, Nest Hub, Chromecasts and cast groups appear automatically — new devices just show up.

🔗
Sync groups

Group any speakers; change one volume anywhere — in this app, on your phone, or on the speaker itself — and the rest follow instantly.

⚖️
Sync Gain balancing

Set a per-device 0–200% gain so quiet corners get boosted and loud rooms stay tame, while the group still moves as one.

📋
System tray presets

Right-click the tray icon and set any group to 5%…100% in two clicks, plus play / pause / next / previous.

⏰
Scheduler

Quiet the house at bedtime: weekly day-and-time rules that set volumes for groups or single devices.

📺
More than Google

Also controls Roku TVs, Yamaha MusicCast receivers, LG webOS TVs and Optoma projectors, in the same sync groups.

Roku is tested on real hardware. The Yamaha, LG and Optoma backends are written to spec but untested — reports welcome.

See it in action

Straight from the app — a real house with twenty Cast devices.

Sync groups that actually stay in sync

Tick the speakers you want to move together. When any member changes — from the app, the Google Home app, or a tap on the speaker — the others are set to match within a moment. Groups are yours, independent of Google’s cast groups, and each one has its own slider and media controls.

Whole-house volume from the tray

You don’t need to open the window. Right-click the tray icon, pick a group, pick a level. 5% steps, plus play, pause, next and previous for whatever is casting.

Schedules for bedtime and mornings

Weekly rules with a time and day checkboxes: drop “Everything” to 50% at 10 pm on school nights, bring the kitchen back up in the morning. Each rule can target several groups or devices at different levels, and toggles on and off with one checkbox.

Set it and forget it

Start with Windows, minimised to the tray. Optional automatic updates from GitHub releases. Export your devices, names, groups and schedules to a JSON file and import them on another PC.
